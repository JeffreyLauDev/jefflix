## [2.0.0] — 2026-11-08

First public release.

### Added
- Apps for macOS (signed and notarized), Windows, Linux, iPhone (TestFlight), LG TV (webOS) and a self-hostable web build.
- Home with a big hero, Continue Watching, newest additions, Top 10 and poster rows; Shows, Movies, MV and My List tabs.
- Own player: speed, quality switch with automatic transcode fallback, episode row with live previews, next episode, AirPlay / cast.
- My List synced with Jellyfin favourites.
- Search with an on-screen keyboard for remotes.
- TV sign-in with a QR code and Jellyfin Quick Connect.
